# Webhost Billing v0.1.0-alpha.5

This release delivers the accumulated hosting, domain, support and reporting
work since alpha.4, plus the full populated upgrade and safer SSH failure path.
It retains one business and one locked base currency per installation.

## Main changes

- Review/pay prorated hosting upgrades and reserve period-end downgrades.
- Track administrator-confirmed domain facts, renewal terms, invoices/reminders,
  paid approval and uncertain-operation reconciliation.
- Route tickets through departments, exchange private screened files and accept
  signed customer email replies through a separately trusted gateway.
- View hosting disk/bandwidth usage, sell fixed-price configuration choices, and
  track manual completion of recurring add-on cancellations.
- Review seven operational/financial reports and preserve product identity on
  future invoice lines without relabelling historical cash.
- Retain merchant-bound SSLCOMMERZ checkout/refund uncertainty and exact replay
  handling. Genuine sandbox and live payment activation remain separate work.

## Alpha.4 upgrade

The published alpha.4 baseline has 46 migrations and 60 application tables.
Alpha.5 has 58 migrations and 74 application tables, plus the migration ledger.
The twelve added migrations are:

1. `20261105000100_hosting_upgrades`
2. `20261105000200_scheduled_hosting_downgrades`
3. `20261105000300_domain_fact_synchronization`
4. `20261105000400_domain_renewal_billing`
5. `20261105000500_paid_domain_renewal_execution`
6. `20261105000600_support_departments`
7. `20261106000100_private_ticket_attachments`
8. `20261106000200_support_email_replies`
9. `20261107000100_ssl_checkout_recovery`
10. `20261107000200_hosting_configuration_choices`
11. `20261107000300_invoice_product_attribution`
12. `20261107000400_domain_invoice_attribution`

The [local rehearsal](../BILLING_UPGRADE_REHEARSAL.md) checks the populated
46→58 transition, preserved history, pending settlement and real failed-migration
refusal. It does not test every installed old binary or replace a real backup.

Use the [dedicated SSH handoff](../SSH_DEPLOYMENT_HANDOFF.md) after release checks.
The script verifies an encrypted pre-change backup, stops application writers,
applies migrations and recreates matching services. A failure/interruption leaves
`deployment-held`; do not clear it or restart old code without the documented
compatibility/recovery decision. Keep failed data and financial/provider evidence.

## Operator follow-up

- Review/reconfirm legacy domain terms and current facts before future billing;
  retain existing issued invoices and payment references.
- Review pending payments, uncertain remote operations, due downgrades, cancelled
  add-on resource removal and purchased configuration fulfillment.
- Existing tickets move into Support with their original conversation intact;
  review staff memberships before narrowing access.
- Keep live registrar renewal dispatch, support email and file intake closed
  until their specific provider/gateway/scanner acceptance is complete. Committed
  defaults do not prove installed flags. Manual bank payment review remains valid.
- Historical income without evidence remains unattributed. Usage is informational,
  forecasts are not collected cash, and manual acknowledgements are staff records.
